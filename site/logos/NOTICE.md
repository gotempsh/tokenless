# Service logos

The SVG files in this folder are copied unchanged from
`broker-ui/src/assets/logos/`, which `tools/catalog/generate.py` builds.
They identify the services the built-in templates work with. Each logo is
a trademark of its owner and implies no endorsement.

All of them come from svgl:

- Source: https://github.com/pheralb/svgl
- Licence: MIT License, Copyright (c) 2022 Pablo Hdez
- Logos: anthropic, cloudflare, discord, github, gitlab, huggingface,
  linear, openai, sentry, slack, stripe, supabase, twilio, vercel

`<key>.dark.svg` is the variant for dark backgrounds. Where svgl had none,
the catalogue generator made it from the same artwork with its dark fill
set to white.

The full notice, with the licence text, is
`broker-ui/src/assets/logos/NOTICE.md` in the repository.
